Privacy Policy for SVG Inserter

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Data Collection and Use

The "SVG Inserter" Google Docs Add-on only requires access to the specific Google Document you are actively working in to insert generated images. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data, document contents, or email address to any external servers.

2. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

SVG Inserter's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Data Storage

All processing of your SVG code into images happens either securely via temporary processing or directly within your browser. We do not retain any copies of your SVGs, images, or documents.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to outside parties. Your data remains entirely within your Google Workspace environment.

5. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us through our main website.
